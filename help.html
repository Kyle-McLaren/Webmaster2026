Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q1: What is the South Brevard Historical Society?

    • A1: The South Brevard Historical Society is a nonprofit organization headquartered in Harborland, and is dedicated to preserving and sharing the history of Southern Brevard County. We work to protect and preserve important local landmarks and buildings, as well as provide educational resources to the community.

  • Q2: How can I get involved with the SBHS?

    • A2: You can volunteer at events, participate in historical tours, or contribute to our preservation projects. For more information, please check out our Contact Us page.

  • Q3: How can I donate to the SCHS?

    • A3: Donations can be mailed to our PO box: 85200, Harborland, 00123.

  • Q4: Where can I find historical records and resources?

    • A4: Resources are available through the Resources page.
