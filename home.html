South Cypress Historical Society

Preserving and Sharing the Rich History of Southern Cypress County

Welcome to South Cypress's Past

The South Cypress Historical Society is dedicated to preserving the stories, landmarks, and cultural heritage that shaped our communities along Florida’s East Coast. We strive to keep local history alive for current and future generations in honor of past generations.

Explore Our Resources

  • Photographic Archives: Browse rare and historic images of Harborland, Pine Bay, and surrounding areas.
  • Historic Maps: Discover how our region has changed over the centuries.
  • Local Oral Histories: Listen to firsthand accounts from long-time Cypress residents.
  • Research Tools: Access databases, references, and links to regional and state archives.
  • Related Organizations: Meet our partner organizations, who make it their goal to preserve history.
