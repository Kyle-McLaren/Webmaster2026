South Cypress Historical Society

Preserving and Sharing the Rich History of Southern Cypress County

Welcome to South Cypress's Past

The South Cypress Historical Society is dedicated to preserving the stories, landmarks, and cultural heritage that shaped our communities along Florida’s East Coast. We strive to keep local history alive for current and future generations in honor of past generations.

Green Gables at Historic Riverview Village

A stunning, 1896 Queen Anne–style home right on the water. Open every Saturday from 10 AM to 2 PM, this house is a great stop to experience the Wells Family's history firsthand!
