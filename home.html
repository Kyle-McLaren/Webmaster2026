South Brevard's Hub of History

Preserving and Sharing the Rich History of Southern Brevard County

Welcome to South Brevard's Past

The South Brevard Historical Society is dedicated to preserving the stories, landmarks, and cultural heritage that shaped our communities along FCHS’s East Coast. We strive to keep local history alive for current and future generations in honor of past generations.

Featured Community Resources

Green Gables at Historic Riverview Village

A stunning, 1896 Queen Anne–style home right on the water. Open every Saturday from 10 AM to 2 PM, this house is a great stop to experience the Wells Family's history firsthand!

Learn More about the Green Gables!

Rossetter House in Historic Rocky Waters

James Rossetter's house provides a snapshot of life in early Rocky Waters, featuring all orignal fixtures and artifacts. This house is for the real history buffs!

Learn More about the Rossetter House!

Liberty Bell Museum within Wells Park

No need to travel when a Liberty Bell replica is located here in Harborland! Operated by Honor America, this museum features military history with a full scale replica of the Liberty Bell!

Learn More about the Liberty Bell Museum!
