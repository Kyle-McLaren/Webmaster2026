El Dorado Hills, California

Our first and home location in El Dorado Hills, California was established in 2017. Located in the peaceful town center, we worked hard to foster an affable environment through our rustic decor and architecture along with unparalleled hospitality.

Sacramento, California

Paying homage to California’s beautiful capital city, our second location– opened in 2021– is Sacramento, California. Designed by the wonderful Bryn Reilly, she combined mid-century modern and rustic decor to create a true Sacramento feel. Like our other locations, Wheat & Gold will continue to facilitate a culture of respect and courtesy for the best dining experience.

San Francisco, California (Coming Soon!)

Expanding our horizons to a larger city, we are excited to announce that Wheat & Gold’s San Francisco location is coming soon! This charming restaurant in an equally lovely city will be a stunning addition to our collection of distinguished eateries.
