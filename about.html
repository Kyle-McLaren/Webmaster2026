Who we Are

Welcome to Wheat & Gold, where we pride ourselves on our sustainability and connection to local agriculture. Located in El Dorado Hills, California, our establishment exudes positive energy, reflected in our homey atmosphere and caring workers.

Our Mission

We operate in a manner that consistently uplifts our planet and community. We exhibit this quality by offering solely vegan and vegetarian options, utilizing compost, minimizing food waste, and using solar energy for power. These are just a few of the ways in which we contribute to the global fight for the environment’s protection. On top of all of our practices, farm-to-table is a large part of our mission. Farm-to-table as a restaurant practice means that ingredients used come directly from a farm, without middlemen. In our case, we have our own farm connected to the restaurant (Wheat & Gold Culinary Garden) on which we grow all of our ingredients, ensuring freshness in every meal.
