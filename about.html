South Cypress Historical Society

Preserving and Sharing the Rich History of Southern Cypress County

Who We Are

Here at the South Cypress Historical Society, we celebrate, preserve, and share the unique history of South Cypress County! From the historic neighborhoods to those long-lost forgotten landmarks, we work to make the past accessible and engaging for present and future generations.

Our Mission

  • Collect and protect historical resources of South Cypress.
  • Promote awareness of South Cypress's history.
  • Partner with local museums, organizations, and historians.
  • Preserve stories and landmarks for future generations.

What We Do

  • Partner with local museums and historic properties
  • Provide accessible online resources for researchers and students
  • Host walking tours, community lectures, and preservation events
  • Document and share the county’s changing history
  • Connect residents with the stories behind their cities

How We Started

Back in 1966, our founder Jackson Palmetto withnessed the destruction of one of Harborland's oldest buildings. Coupled with the fact that others too could share the same faith, he founded the Cypress Historical Society, later becoming the South Cypress Historical Society to better suit South Cypress's needs.

Get Involved

Whether you're a longtime resident, a student researcher (we love and need you!), or just curious about South Cypress’s history, we would love to have you join our growing organization. Explore our resources, share your stories, and get involved with one of our projects!
